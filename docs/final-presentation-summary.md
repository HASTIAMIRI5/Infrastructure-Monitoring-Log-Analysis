# Final Presentation Summary

## Executive Summary
Infrastructure monitoring and log analysis are essential operational skills for maintaining reliable systems and services. This lab focuses on detecting resource issues, reviewing logs, and identifying the most probable root causes of service degradation or outages.

## Key Takeaways
- Monitoring provides early insight into resource pressure and service instability
- Logs provide the timeline and evidence needed to explain failures
- Correlating metrics and logs helps isolate the real cause of an issue
- Documentation is critical for communication, follow-up, and remediation tracking

## Presentation Narrative
The lab begins with baseline checks across CPU, memory, disk, and network health. Next, it moves into log review and event correlation, helping participants recognize recurring patterns such as failed service dependencies, memory pressure, or disk exhaustion. Finally, the lab closes with formal documentation of findings and corrective actions, reinforcing the importance of operational reporting.

## Conclusion
This project illustrates how a structured monitoring and incident-analysis workflow improves reliability, reduces downtime, and strengthens troubleshooting capability. It prepares learners to operate effectively in infrastructure support and system administration roles.
